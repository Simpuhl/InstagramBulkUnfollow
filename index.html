Social Unfollower

Find the accounts you follow that don't follow you back, and unfollow them in bulk. Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B, or Cmd+Shift+B on Mac), then drag a button onto it.

Instagram Unfollowers

Instagram Unfollowers

  1. Open www.instagram.com and log in.
  2. Click the bookmark, then Run Scan.

TikTok Unfollowers

TikTok Unfollowers

  1. Open www.tiktok.com, log in, and go to your profile.
  2. Click your Following count so the list opens.
  3. Click the bookmark, then Scan.

Everything runs in your own browser; nothing is sent anywhere else. Use at your own risk: unfollowing a lot at once can get your account temporarily blocked. Instagram tool based on InstagramUnfollowers by David Arroyo (MIT).
